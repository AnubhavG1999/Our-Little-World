# Our Little World

A shared, cozy pixel-art room where Pratiksha and Anubhav, who live apart, spend time together, leave each other notes, and look after a cat. Pratiksha made the original game; this glossary keeps her names for things.

## Language

### People

**Pratiksha**:
The girlfriend who created the game, and the player of the girl Character.
_Avoid_: g, her, player 1

**Anubhav**:
The player of the boy Character.
_Avoid_: b, Him, player 2

**You**:
On each screen, the player looking at it. Labels and notices call the viewer "You" and the other player by name, so the same event reads "You fed the cat" on one screen and "Anubhav fed the cat" on the other.

**Character**:
The pixel figure standing in for one player in the Room. Each player moves only their own Character; the other one shows where the other player is.
_Avoid_: avatar, sprite, player

### The room

**Room**:
The one shared scene the game takes place in.
_Avoid_: map, level, world

**Hotspot**:
A clickable part of the Room's painting — the mailbox, the TV, the coffee table, the left-hand menu items.
_Avoid_: button, spot

**Furniture**:
The bed, sofa and desk: Hotspots that offer something to do there.

**Pair moment**:
Something both Characters do together, shown as one shared picture — a kiss, hug, cuddle, sitting together, and so on. Either player can start or end one, and both see it.
_Avoid_: pair, action, interaction

**Held pose**:
A pose that lasts until its player moves again, like sitting, typing at the laptop, or a cuddle.
_Avoid_: hold, sticky pose

**Cat**:
The grey Persian who lives in the Room. Where she wanders is each player's own; her food Bowl is shared.

**Plant**:
A shared plant in the Room that grows while either player waters it daily and droops when both forget.

**Emote**:
A short reaction (heart, laugh, wave, sleepy) that pops over a Character's head.

**Day count**:
The number of days since the couple's anniversary, shown on the Room's "Day" badge.
_Avoid_: day 1, streak

**Outfit**:
The colours a player picks for their Character's top and bottoms at the Room's mirror. "Original" means Pratiksha's colours.
_Avoid_: skin, costume, clothes

### Views

**Room view**:
Pratiksha's original framing: the whole Room on one screen.
_Avoid_: desktop mode, full view

**Gamer view**:
A close-up that follows the player's own Character around the Room, with a joystick and hotbar; the default on phones held upright.
_Avoid_: mobile mode, zoomed mode, portrait mode

### Keepsakes

**Snapshot**:
A picture the in-game Camera takes of the Room as it is right now, framed like a polaroid with the date.
_Avoid_: screenshot, photo

**Memory**:
Anything kept in Memories: a Snapshot, or a real photo one of the players uploaded.
_Avoid_: picture, post, image

**Pin**:
A place on Our Map, marked either "been there" or "want to go".
_Avoid_: marker, location

**Plan**:
One thing on the Future Plans list the couple wants to do together.
_Avoid_: goal, task, bucket-list item

**Open-when note**:
A note one player hides in the jar for the other; Surprise Me draws one at random.
_Avoid_: surprise, jar note, gift

### Music

**Our playlist**:
The one Spotify playlist the couple shares, played from the Music panel.
_Avoid_: radio, queue

**Now playing**:
The song a player is listening to on Spotify right now, shown as a ♪ chip over their Character.
_Avoid_: status, scrobble

**Listen together**:
Starting the same song on both players' Spotify apps at once. It is a nudge, not a perfectly synced stream.
_Avoid_: Jam, group session, sync play

### Talking

**Message**:
A short chat line between the two players.
_Avoid_: chat, text, DM

**Letter**:
A longer note kept separately from Messages so it is never pushed out by chatter.
_Avoid_: long message, note

**Speech bubble**:
Words floating over a Character's head in the Room — their lines in a Pair moment, or a Message as it arrives.
_Avoid_: say panel, caption
